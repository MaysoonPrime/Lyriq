📋 How to Use Sheet Music Scanner:

  1. Upload or drag a photo of sheet music
  2. Click "Analyze Sheet Music" to process it
  3. The AI will extract the notes and rhythms
  4. Click "Play Preview" to hear the melody
  5. Go to the Practice tab to sing along!

💡 Note: This demo shows the workflow. For full functionality, you'll need to add an AI API key.
